# Outbound.ai — Setup Guide

## What you're deploying
An AI-powered outbound system that:
- Captures leads from your website + Google Ads automatically
- Scores every lead 0-100 with AI
- Enrolls them in email sequences automatically
- Sends personalized, AI-written follow-ups on a schedule
- Detects replies and classifies intent
- Tracks opens, clicks, replies
- Shows everything in a live dashboard

See full setup instructions in the repo.
